Lists that fit no archetype, and players with no decklist, are not given a row or column. They still count in "vs field".

Each cell is the row deck's win rate against the column deck, with the number of matches underneath. Byes and awarded losses are left out. Drawn matches count toward the sample but not the rate. Cells under the fade threshold are too small to trust.

Ink pairs

Share by round

The highest-placed players on each ink pair, in Play Hub's standings order (tiebreakers applied). "Behind" is match points behind that ink pair's leader.

🔒 means nobody else on the ink pair can still finish above that player. 🏃 means still in the running: they can finish top of their ink pair, or at least draw level on points.

Players and drops

"Left" counts players who were paired that round and never again. At the end of a phase that mostly means missing the cut.
