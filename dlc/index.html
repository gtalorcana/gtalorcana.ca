Share is of players paired in the selected rounds. Win rates leave out mirrors, byes and awarded losses; draws are listed separately.

Share by round

The highest-placed players on each ink pair, in Play Hub's standings order (tiebreakers applied). "Behind" is match points behind that ink pair's leader; "Best case" is the most points a player can still reach before the next cut.

Locked means nobody else on the ink pair can finish above the leader, whatever happens. Tiebreak only means a rival can draw level on points but not pass. This is points arithmetic, not a prediction, and it assumes best-of goes to the highest final placing — so a player who reaches the next phase finishes above everyone who does not.

Players and drops

"Left" counts players who were paired that round and never again. At the end of a phase that mostly means missing the cut.
